* Delivered PLC and HMI applications for oil & gas facilities in Ecuador, California, and Texas, from specification through field commissioning.
* Migrated critical Safety Instrumented Systems (SIS) from legacy PLC-5 hardware to modern ControlLogix platforms, converting safety logic and validating it against the original cause-and-effect matrices.
* Integrated field equipment with PLCs over Modbus for real-time monitoring and control from a central SCADA system.
