* Built a 3D-CNN lens-smudge detector for a mixed-reality headset camera pipeline, exploiting a smudge's persistence across frames to separate fixed optical contamination from moving scene content, safeguarding the stream quality that on-device perception and tracking depend on.
* Owned the supporting data pipeline end to end: curated and annotated a large-scale video dataset and automated preprocessing and augmentation in Python (PyTorch, OpenCV).
* Internship supported by the GEM Fellowship (Apple Inc. as employer fellow).
